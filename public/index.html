Frequently asked questions

How does the transfer actually work?

We convert USDT to USD as part of moving your money. That's what lets us settle transfers quickly and quote a live, transparent rate instead of the delays and hidden markups of traditional bank transfers.

Is this safe?

We confirm the rate with you before you send anything, and payouts are sent as soon as your transfer is confirmed on-chain. Start with a small test amount if you're new to us.

How fast is payout?

Usually within minutes of your transfer confirming, during normal hours.

What currencies do you support?

Message us your currency — we support a range of local payout options.
