Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Light Money Transfer ("we," "us," "our"), operator of lightmt.com ("the Service"), collects and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly when you contact us to arrange a transfer — such as your name, phone number or messaging handle, and transaction details (amount, currencies, recipient information). If you message us on WhatsApp, your phone number and messages are recorded so our team can process your transfer and respond to you.

2. What we don't collect

Browsing our public website does not set any tracking or advertising cookies, and we don't run analytics scripts that follow you across other sites. The only cookie used anywhere on this site is a technical session cookie set when our staff log into the internal dashboard — it has nothing to do with site visitors and isn't set unless someone actually logs in.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to process your money transfer, communicate with you about it, maintain accurate transaction records, and improve our service.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers as necessary to complete your transfer, or when required by law or a valid legal request.

5. Data security

All connections to our site are encrypted (HTTPS). Staff account passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing, never in plain text. Access to customer and transaction records is restricted to authenticated staff, and all changes to that data are logged.

6. Data retention

We retain transaction and contact records for as long as necessary for business operations and applicable legal/record-keeping requirements.

7. Your rights

You can contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to request corrections or deletion where applicable. We'll respond as promptly as we can.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request regarding your data? Reach us at support@kstorez.com.

This page is a plain-language summary provided for transparency and does not constitute a substitute for professional legal advice.
